About

Designing with comfort and character

I’m a fashion designer based in Payyanur, Kerala, and I’ve been designing and stitching since 2019. I love creating custom ladies’ dresses, baby and kids’ frocks, birthday party wear, sleepwear and smart garments that feel as good as they look.

My work starts with trend research and careful sketching, then moves through pattern making and garment construction, with model fittings to make sure every piece is comfortable. I also work with suppliers to keep projects within budget.

As a KTET-qualified educator, I’ve taught practical tailoring, pattern making and garment construction to students, and I enjoy sharing the craft as much as practising it.
